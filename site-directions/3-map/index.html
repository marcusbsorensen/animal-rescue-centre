What children do in A.R.C.

Every job is about looking after someone. Nothing is ever hurt, and nothing is ever rushed.

1Rescue scared animals

A phone call says an animal needs help. Children drive out, choose the right travel crate and bring it back gently.

2Feed, heal and walk them

Animals get hungry, tired, grubby and bored. Children feed, groom, play with and walk them, and take poorly ones to the vet.

3Find them forever homes

When an animal is ready, families who want that kind of pet come to the adoption office. Children choose a home and say goodbye at the hand-over.

4Drive the van safely

The van runs on real-feeling local roads. Children pick a sensible gear for the road, keep left and overtake only where it is allowed. Rush, and the speed camera flashes. No one is ever hurt: the van brakes itself.

5Learn real animal care

The kitchen game teaches the right food for each animal: hay and greens for rabbits, seeds for parrots, meaty food for hedgehogs, never bread and milk. New species bring "Did you know?" facts.

6Grow the centre

Cats and dogs first; then foxes and rabbits, bats and parrots, snakes and hedgehogs. More room, apprentices and new places on the map as the centre grows.
