A.R.C. master site plan v3 — viewing-dome path

Long NNW-oriented plot between Viking Close + Canute Road. Hedgehog + raccoon zones now run parallel down the plot, with a central path between them. Low Art-Deco viewing domes punctuate the path at 3 points — walkers peep through to watch foxes running through the tunnel below. The aviary sits on the main-building roof; snakes have a wall hatch to a sunny basking patch.

Tier 1 paints: the entire scene, but only with parking + main building + lawn + quiet garden + the central path with viewing domes visible. Empty painted ground sits where T2-T6 land.
Tier 2 (hedgehog): adds painted leaf piles + log corners around the three trees in the LEFT half of the small-mammal zone. No new building footprint.
Tier 3 (raccoon): paints the climbing-structure enclosure into the RIGHT half of the small-mammal zone.
Tier 4 (parrot): aviary mesh-dome appears on the main-building roof + a parrot visible flying through the open ceiling hatch.
Tier 5 (snake): wall hatch visible on east face of main building + small basking patch beside.
Tier 6 (fox): a fox visible through one of the path's viewing domes (running in the tunnel below) + tunnel-exit emergence + fox in the southern pen.
Viewing-dome design: low Art Deco aircraft-canopy aesthetic — polished metal frame, glass top, ~1.2m diameter, set flush into the path so kids stop and crouch over them. Runs the full length of the small-mammal zone (3 domes) over the fox tunnel below.
