## Key landmarks on this map

**Coast (north):**
- Birchington Minnis Bay (A.R.C. neighbourhood — see `birchie-minnis-bay/`)
- Grenham Bay
- Epple Bay Beach
- Westgate & Birchington Golf Club (top-right corner)

**Village centre (see `birchie-centre/`):**
- Birchington-on-Sea train station, Medical Centre, Wimpy, Sainsbury's Local, Alpha House Dental Practice, Sexy Shooz
- The Cow Shed (pub/restaurant — optional cameo)
- Old Coach House (flavour landmark)

**West entrance (south-west on the main road out):**
- **Esso** — the real petrol station. In-game: the petrol-station destination. Naming TBD (generic or keep-Esso; recommend generic).
- **Fishbone Grill** — the real car-wash location. In-game: where the wavy-arm inflatable car-wash lives.

**South / inland — Wyx Park estate area:**
- Recreation Ground (top of the Wyx area)
- Birchington CE Primary School (nearby)
- Quex Barn (estate outbuilding)
- Quex Glamping (bottom of the Wyx area — marks the southern edge)
- The Carriage Livery at Tilleys Glamping (far south-east)
- Crape Park (south-east, part of the estate edge)
- Powell-Cotton Museum — **dropped from game (real museum has taxidermy, tonally wrong)**.

**Non-Wyx inland:**
- Jungle Jim's Ltd — real softplay centre.
- **Rhubarb's Rock On Music Academy** — **pinned by Marcus 2026-04-24**, next to Jungle Jim's. Where apprentice Rhubarb learns guitar, drums, keyboard. Keep the real name (it's perfect). Supply-run destination with thematic tie-in to the heavy-metal Supply Run music.
- Shuttered Dreams (possible pub/restaurant — optional cameo).
