## A.R.C. plot

On the map: the **large open green space immediately south of The Dip**, bounded by The Parade (north), the residential streets of King's Avenue / Ethelbert Road area (east), and Dane Road / Horsa Road area (south).

The plot's scale supports a sprawling sanctuary layout:
- Main building along the northern edge with sea views toward The Parade.
- Outbuildings and paddocks wrapping south.
- Apprentice bothy + Depot shed at the back.

## Key real-world landmarks used

- **The Dip** — multifunctional sunken sports ground immediately north of A.R.C.; pet-show venue.
- **Minnis Bay beach + paddling pool** — tidal dog beach + paddling pool for scared/recovering dogs (~1 min walk north).
- **The Parade** — seafront road with rainbow beach huts.
- **Dapper Dogs** — real dog groomer; keep the name, possible apprentice-owned business or grooming errand destination.
- **Wagtails Cafe & Bar** — real café with a perfect-for-the-game name; **keep the name verbatim** (exception to the generic-shop-names rule).
- **Kearns Hall** — community hall; possible low-tier pet-show venue.
- **The Bay United Reformed Church** — flavour landmark.
- **Bay Lodge** — possible adopter household / apprentice residence.
- **Waves Bed & Breakfast** — flavour landmark, possibly where visitors stay.
