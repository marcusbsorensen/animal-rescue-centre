Design decisions — why these choices
  • ≥60px touch targets: kids have less fine motor precision.
  • Labels always visible for ages 8+ — icon-only is too opaque.
  • Active tab raised + coloured — spatial cue stronger than colour alone.
  • Max 5 tabs — scanning above 5 taxes short-term memory.
  • HUD split into identity vs. status reduces visual noise.
  • Speech bubble attached to animal (not a side card) so cause/effect connects.
  • Drop shadows on every animal — #1 "looks amateur" tell if missing.
  • Warm palette, no pure saturated reds — friendly coral & green.
  • No emojis in final build — these mocks are placeholders; PNG icons replace.
