=== WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE ===
This is the whole point of using your own outputs as the source. For every file:
- the silhouette and outline, pixel for pixel as near as you can hold it
- the camera — if the source looks straight down, the output looks straight down; if
  the source is a side elevation, the output is a side elevation. Do not tilt, rotate,
  re-angle or "improve" any viewpoint. Perspective is handled later in code, not by you.
- which way the vehicle faces
- the canvas size, the position on the canvas, and the transparent background
- the line-and-wash style, the weight of the ink line, the paper texture
Only the colours, panels, badges and lettering change.

=== COLOUR WARNING ===
In an earlier batch a blue car came back grey because the brief said "muted" and that
was read as "drain the colour". These are children's book illustrations: the washes are
SOFT, not WEAK. Keep the saturation of the master. Muted means no neon and no plastic
sheen — it does not mean grey.

=== SIDE-ONLY vs OVERHEAD ===
Door badges and flank lettering live on the SIDES of a vehicle. Seen from directly
above you would not see them. Do not paste a door badge onto a roof. On an overhead
view the flank decoration appears only as a sliver along the outer edge, if at all.
