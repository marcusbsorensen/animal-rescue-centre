> 10 sample prompts for Lily to review before committing to the full ~60-generation batch.

## Master Style Prefix

> Prepend to ALL prompts:

```
Children's storybook illustration style, hand-drawn with visible crayon and pencil texture,
slightly wobbly lines, flat colours with soft cel-shading, warm saturated palette across the
full spectrum, friendly chibi proportions with large heads and expressive eyes, clean
transparent background, designed to look like an 8-year-old's favourite picture book.
```
