What players do in A.R.C.

Every job is about looking after an animal.

  • Ages 5 to 7 with a grown-up's help
  • 8 and up on their own
  • Teens and adults welcome too

Rescue animals in need

A call comes in. Drive out and choose the right crate. Bring the animal home.

Care for every animal

Feed and groom them. Play and go for walks. Poorly ones visit the vet.

Find them forever homes

Families visit the adoption office. Choose the best home for each animal.

Drive the rescue van

Pick your gear and keep left. Go too fast and the camera flashes!

Learn real animal care

Cook the right food for each animal. Hedgehogs never get bread and milk!

Grow your centre

Cats and dogs come first. Then foxes and rabbits and bats and more.
